Orihon Support

Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome.

Write to support@orihon.app. One person reads the mail, so a reply can take a day or two. If something looks wrong, a screenshot helps.

Where is my book kept?

On your iPhone. If iCloud sync is on, it is also kept in your own private iCloud, so it follows you to a new phone. You can switch sync off in App Settings. The developer cannot see your book either way.

How do I get everything out?

Turn to the back cover and choose Export Everything. Orihon saves one folder to Files with your pages, photos and writing.

I turned on the lock and Touch ID or Face ID isn't working

The lock falls back to your iPhone passcode. Orihon has no password of its own, so there is nothing separate to forget.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone Duo, and iPhones running iOS 27 or later.

I'm testing Orihon through TestFlight

Thank you. You can send feedback from the TestFlight app or by email, whichever is easier. Please don't keep anything irreplaceable only in Orihon until it reaches the App Store.
