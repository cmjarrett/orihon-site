Lune Support

Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome.

Write to support@orihon.app. One person reads the mail, so a reply can take a day or two. If something looks wrong, a screenshot helps, and so does saying which source you were playing: Apple Music or your own server.

What does Lune play?

Your Apple Music library and the Apple Music catalog, with your own Apple Music subscription. Or your own Navidrome or other Subsonic server. One source plays at a time; switch between them in ••• › Sources. Without an Apple Music subscription, Lune plays the music synced to your iPhone.

Can I download Apple Music songs to play offline?

Not in Lune. Apple doesn't let other apps download from Apple Music. Songs you've downloaded in Apple's Music app play in Lune without a network. Music from your own server can be downloaded in Lune: albums, playlists or single songs.

Does Lune scrobble to Last.fm or ListenBrainz?

Lune sends your plays to your own server when Scrobble plays is on (••• › Sources). Navidrome can pass them on to Last.fm or ListenBrainz if you set that up on the server. With Apple Music, Lune sends plays nowhere.

Does Lune work with CarPlay?

Not yet. Lune doesn't have its own CarPlay screens. They will come in an update once Apple allows it.

I bought the Full Pass. How do I get it on another iPhone?

Open ••• › Customize and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. The Full Pass is shared with your family through Family Sharing. Playing, search, both sources and every Apple Music feature are free either way.

My server won't connect

Check the address opens in Safari on the same iPhone, including https:// and any port. If you reach your server through a VPN such as Tailscale, switch the VPN on first. Lune never retries a failed sign-in by itself. Navidrome blocks an address for a while after several wrong passwords in a row, so wait a minute before trying again.

Where is my information kept?

On your iPhone. Lune has no account and collects nothing. It talks only to Apple and to the server you sign in to. The privacy policy says what goes where.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone Duo, and iPhones running iOS 27 or later.

I'm testing Lune through TestFlight

Thank you. You can send feedback from the TestFlight app or by email, whichever is easier.
