iXoon Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

iXoon is a music player for iPhone Duo and iPhone. It plays your own Apple Music subscription and, if you do not have one, your own Navidrome or Subsonic server. It is a player over music you already have, not a music service of its own. This page says plainly what the app does with your information.

iXoon is a working title. When the app has its final name, this page will move to a new address and the old one will point to it.

The short version

  • iXoon collects nothing about you. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer receives no data from the app.
  • The app talks to two places only: Apple, when you play Apple Music, and your own server, if you add one. Nothing goes anywhere else.
  • The app only ever sends what playback needs and what you ask it to do, such as marking a favourite.
  • There are no accounts of the app's own. You sign in to your music, not to iXoon.

Apple Music

When you play Apple Music, the app uses Apple's MusicKit. Your requests, such as a search, a browse of your library or a track to play, go from your iPhone to Apple, and Apple handles them under Apple's Privacy Policy. iOS asks your permission before the app can see your music library. The developer never sees your Apple Account, your library or what you play. iXoon never sells or gates access to Apple Music; you pay Apple for your subscription and the app plays it.

Your own server

If you add a Navidrome or Subsonic server, the address, username and password or token you enter are stored in the iPhone Keychain and sent only to that server. They go nowhere else. The app sends your server what playback needs: which track to stream, at what quality, and the artwork to show. It also sends the actions you take yourself, such as marking a star or favourite.

If you switch scrobbling on in the app, it tells your server which tracks you played and when. Your server may pass those plays on to ListenBrainz, Last.fm or another service, but only if you set that up on the server. That is outside the app and under the terms of the service you chose.

iCloud

If the app syncs its own settings between your devices, it uses your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. The developer cannot see it. Apple runs iCloud under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your music and your server credentials are never put in iCloud by the app.

What never happens

  • No analytics or tracking software of any kind, from the developer or anyone else.
  • No advertising.
  • No accounts beyond your music sources and your own iCloud.
  • No artwork or artist pictures fetched from the open web. Artwork comes from Apple Music or from your own server.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. The developer collects nothing, so there is nothing to sell or share.

Crash reports reach the developer only if you have chosen, in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, to share them with app developers. That is Apple's setting, and the reports Apple passes on carry no personal information.

Purchases

You buy the app, and anything in it, through Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

iXoon collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to what leaves your iPhone is published here, with a new date, in the same release as the change. The app will never start collecting your information without saying so first.
